## The entry

> Error E12: unbalanced load. I have spent 40 minutes shaking one sock. Add a towel or accept the noise.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the line to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`). The output is a single line of 102 characters, within the 200-character limit.

## Why this line

Unbalanced load is the most honest fault a washing machine ever reports — every owner has heard the drum thump itself around the laundry room at midnight. The joke is the machine's bookkeeping: it has spent 40 minutes of its life shaking one sock, a job no one asked it to do and no one can win. The punchline is a deadpan ultimatum with no sympathy left in it — add a towel, or live with the noise. The machine never tries to be funny; it is simply tired, and that is exactly what makes it funny.
